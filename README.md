## Executive Summary
Douyin social commerce runs at high velocity, where campaign success depends on tight synchronization between creative live-stream production, Gift-With-Purchase (GWP) manufacturing lead times, host compliance, and real-time warehouse stock. 

As a lifelong LEGO® builder and Project Management specialist, I engineered this interactive **Douyin Live-Commerce Command Center** to demonstrate how rigorous critical-path scheduling, SKU inventory control, and cross-functional governance directly elevate Douyin channel conversion.

---

## Key Modules & Job Responsibility Alignment

1. **Interactive LEGO® Live Studio Brick Builder (Hero Experience)**
   * A playable 5-step brick-snapping assembly that constructs a miniature LEGO® Douyin Live Studio & Gift Box model piece by piece, reflecting hands-on product passion and structured execution.
2. **Campaign & Assortment Stock Sync**
   * Tracks upcoming mega-campaigns (618 Mid-Year Festival, Double 11, Chinese New Year Gifting, Super Brand Day) and aligns promotional calendars with SKU and GWP stock buffers across Commercial and Supply Chain teams.
3. **GWP & Festival Gift-Box Critical Path Tracker**
   * Monitors end-to-end production milestones (`Concept` -> `Sampling` -> `Costing & Finance` -> `Packaging Assembly` -> `Warehouse Stock-In` -> `Live-Stream Ready`) with automated bottleneck flagging and vertical 9:16 screen vs. retail shelf packaging checks.
4. **9:16 Vertical Live Studio & Host Compliance Scanner**
   * Standardizes studio backdrops, props, 3-second scroll-stopping hooks, and key selling points for in-house and agency hosts, paired with a real-time **China Advertising Law** scanner that flags prohibited superlatives before broadcast.
5. **Live Funnel & GWP Burn-Rate Simulator**
   * Models live-room exposure, yellow-cart click-through rate (CTR), order conversion, and GMV while alerting teams before GWP stockouts occur mid-stream.
6. **Operation Partner (TP Agency) SLA Governance**
   * Tracks external agency uptime, host talk-track adherence, visual guideline QA, and timestamped action directives.
